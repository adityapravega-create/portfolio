// 01 · My Story

The Engineer Behind The Numbers

Being surrounded by a family of engineers, I was drawn to cars and aircraft from childhood. I watched my father explain engine working principles — and in sixth grade wrote "I will become an Engineer" on my report card and meant every word.

Formula 1 revealed the magic of aerodynamics — an inverted wing gluing a car to tarmac at unimaginable speed. That became my obsession: where science meets artistry, every curve holding the power to dictate performance.

At VIT Vellore I joined India's legacy Formula Student team Pravega Racing. Some nights were full workshops — manufacturing carbon fibre bodywork by hand, running simulations, collecting lap data to shave fractions of a second while the college slept.

Industry at Tata Motors and Adani Defence applied my skills to real problems. At Imperial College London, my MSc pushed the boundary between numerical simulation and experimental validation — with Formula One as the destination.

Final Year Project · Top Result

FSAE Rear Wing Mount — Generative Design & CFD

Pravega Racing / VIT · Sep–Nov 2024

Designed and optimised an FSAE rear wing mount for PRV 25 using Fusion 360 generative design and STAR-CCM+ CFD. Swan neck vs lower mount configurations evaluated. Above-wing (swan neck) selected — 50% mass reduction and 24% better aerodynamic efficiency, validated at a 2.5× safety factor.

  • Swan neck and lower mount configurations modelled and compared on aerodynamic and structural criteria.
  • Generative design in Fusion 360 driven by load cases from the CFD pressure field.
  • Above-wing mount selected on the combined mass, downforce and structural result.
  • Final design validated at a 2.5× safety factor, then run back through CFD: 24% better aerodynamic efficiency.
  • Thesis returned a 3% similarity score on an industry-grade Turnitin check.

RANS Equations in Moving Reference Frame

ESDU & Imperial College London · Feb 2026 – Sep 2026

Derived the full 2D RANS equations from a stationary to a Moving Reference Frame via transformation axes, and adapted the Spalart-Allmaras and k-ω turbulence models for unsteady propeller flow — in collaboration with ESDU.

  • Spalart-Allmaras and k-ω turbulence models adapted for unsteady propeller flow fields.
  • Run in collaboration with the Engineering Science Data Unit (ESDU) toward an industry-standard reference publication.
